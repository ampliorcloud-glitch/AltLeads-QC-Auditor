Amplior Quality Manual v1.0
Date: March 10, 2026
Version Notes: First draft for pilot on 2-3 campaigns. Editable for feedback and refinement.
A. Purpose
Amplior needs this Quality Manual to establish a comprehensive Lead Quality + Process Quality + Pipeline Quality framework. It drives better client leads, supports stronger CPL/per-lead pricing, trains teams on "good work," enables scientific performance reviews, and builds toward a single quality score per lead/meeting.
Quality is defined as the end-to-end strength of the lead/meeting for the client—not just call interaction quality. It emphasizes business outcomes like reaching the right decision-maker, clear pitches, real engagement, proper qualification, meeting clarity, follow-ups, CRM accuracy, and sales potential.
B. Scope
Quality applies across all touchpoints: calls, emails, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, meeting generation, qualification, follow-ups, CRM/MIS updates, and handover to client/sales team.
C. Definitions
Term	Definition
Lead	Prospect contact with initial interest signal (e.g., conversation started).
Qualified Lead	Meets ICP, shows need, basic qualification data captured.
High-Quality Lead	Score 85+; right person, strong engagement, full qualification, multi-channel support, high conversion potential.
Push-Through Meeting	Forced booking without genuine interest (typically <70 score).
Engaged Prospect	Asks questions, shares info, accepts follow-ups/responsive.
Right Decision-Maker	Budget authority/approver; vs. Influencer (introducer, no budget).
Meeting Accepted	Calendar invite confirmed by prospect.
Meeting Attended	Prospect shows up with sales rep.
Sales-Qualified Lead (SQL)	Passed to sales with 70+ score, full qualification.
CPL Lead Quality Standard	Average score >75 for cost-per-lead viability.
D. Lead Lifecycle
Quality checks are linked to each stage for consistent evaluation.
Stage	Description	Quality Checks
Prospect Identified	Verify ICP fit.	Company relevant? Industry/turnover/location fit?
Outreach Initiated	Initial contact via email/LinkedIn/WhatsApp.	Familiarity built? Not cold? Channel appropriate?
Conversation Established	Introduce services, value prop, qualifying questions.	Right person? Interest/need signals? Basic qual captured?
Meeting Scheduled	Meeting fixed, calendar invite shared.	Clear date/time? All participants included?
Reminder Sent	Pre-meeting reconfirmation.	Attendance confirmed? Agenda/expectations clarified?
Meeting Attended	Meeting occurs with sales rep.	Prospect present? Discussion productive?
Feedback Received	Sales Partner (SP) input on outcome.	Engagement level? Requirement potential?
Opportunity Identified	Assess business potential.	Clear next steps? Viable opportunity?
E. Quality Scorecard / Framework
10-parameter, 100-point system. Multiple factors must align for high quality (e.g., good conversation but wrong person = low score).
Parameter	Description	Max Points	Scoring Guide
Right Prospect/Persona Fit	Right decision-maker/budget holder/influencer/stakeholder?	20	20=DM confirmed; 10=Influencer; 0=Wrong person.
Account/ICP Relevance	Fits sector/size/geography/solution?	10	10=Perfect ICP; 5=Partial; 0=Irrelevant.
Pitch Quality	Clear/relevant value prop?	15	15=Tailored; 10=Standard; 0=Irrelevant.
Interest/Need Signal	Genuine interest/problem awareness?	15	15=Explicit need; 10=Openness; 0=None.
Qualification Depth	Vendor/reqs/timing/budget/process captured?	15	15=All 5 points; 3 pts each.
Engagement Strength	Questions asked/responsive/shared context?	10	10=High; 5=Moderate; 0=Forced.
Multi-Channel Support	Email/LinkedIn/WA/invite/reminder?	5	1 pt per action (max 5).
Meeting Readiness	Clear date/time/accepted invite/show probability?	5	5=Accepted+reminder ack; 0=No invite.
CRM/Data Accuracy	Notes/details/qual points correct?	3	3=Complete; 0=Errors.
Process Adherence	Followed defined steps?	2	2=Full; 0=None.
Total		100	Bands: 85-100=High; 70-84=Good; 50-69=Moderate; <50=Weak.
Usage: Score post-feedback. Average >75 target.
F. Audit Process
•	Who: Agents/TLs (daily self-review), QA (weekly), Managers (monthly).
•	How Often: Daily (10% new leads), Weekly (20% sample), Monthly (50/campaign).
•	Sample Size: 5-10% volume (min 20/team). Stratify by agent/client.
•	Method: Scorecard in Google Sheet; random + low-show/low-conversion priority.
G. Coaching and Correction
Weekly 1:1/group sessions for <75 scores. Format:
